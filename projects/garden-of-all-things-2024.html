Garden of All Things

万物花园

The story

An immersive garden at the meeting point of nature and the female gaze.

The 2024 edition takes the garden as a metaphor for interconnectedness. Through an eco-feminist lens, it explores the bonds between women and nature, weaving natural vitality with inner strength to create an immersive, spiritually resonant experience.

Artists & collaborators
