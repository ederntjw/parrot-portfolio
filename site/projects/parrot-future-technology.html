Parrot & Future Technology

鹦鹉与未来科技

The story

The future, imagined through children's eyes.

Presented at Eslite Bookstore Suzhou as the public-benefit component of Ye Fan's solo exhibition, this children's art project brings together imaginative visions of tomorrow from young people with varied backgrounds. It reflects the foundation's commitment to art education, youth creativity and bringing art into everyday life.
