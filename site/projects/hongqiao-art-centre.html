Hongqiao Art Centre

上海虹桥艺术中心

The story

A new cultural life for the former Tianshan Cinema.

A collaboration with BAU Design Office, the Shanghai Hongqiao Art Centre renewal brings cinema, theatre and shared public space into a connected cultural complex. Distinct volumes are linked by an enclosed plaza, preserving their individual character while encouraging exchange between film, performing arts and different audiences.
